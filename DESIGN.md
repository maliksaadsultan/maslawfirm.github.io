## Direction contract

Thesis: structural clarity connects the logo’s rising arrow to an upward architectural view. Navy and the authentic logo blue distinguish MAS from generic gold-accented legal templates.

Own world: substantial navy fields, white logo space, cool paper reading surfaces, calm serif titles and a practical sans voice. Open service rows replace repeating shadow cards.

Story: understand the firm's scope, identify a relevant service, read preserved guidance, then choose a consultation channel.

First viewport: white logo/navigation header; navy text field at 55% with three-line display and direct action; architectural image at 45%. Mobile stacks text and image with the same priority.

Form: server-rendered static documents, grouped disclosure directory, editorial articles, bounded tables and labeled forms.

Interaction: native menu state, tactile action feedback, finite entrance choreography and explicit enquiry status. Working links, focus and no-JS content matter at every width.
