## Confirmed conflicts

- **ATL frequency, filer guide:** original says Sunday-night updates. FBR announced daily updates under S.R.O. 1638(I)/2024. The new notice links to the [FBR announcement](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/press-release/174151). Confirm latest-year inclusion and surcharge treatment before replacing the original paragraph.
- **Company requirements:** original presents two directors, two shareholders and Rs. 100,000 as minimum requirements. [SECP incorporation guidance](https://www.secp.gov.pk/company-formation/registration-of-company/) distinguishes public, private and single-member structures. The notice explains the examples are not universal. Review capital, liability exceptions and transfer restrictions.
