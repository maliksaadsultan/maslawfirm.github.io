Welcome to MAS Tax Firm

MAS Tax Firm is a premier tax and legal consultancy based in Taxila, Pakistan. We specialize in providing comprehensive business and legal services to clients across Pakistan, including company registration, tax compliance, intellectual property protection, and regulatory services.

Our team of experienced tax professionals and lawyers in Taxila brings deep expertise in Income Tax, Sales Tax, Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA), International Taxation, and corporate law. We understand the unique challenges faced by businesses in Pakistan and provide tailored solutions to meet your specific needs.

Whether you are starting a new business, managing an existing enterprise, or facing complex tax matters, our team is committed to delivering exceptional legal and tax services. We combine technical expertise with practical experience to help our clients navigate Pakistan's regulatory landscape effectively.

Our Key Strengths

At MAS Tax Firm, our strength lies in our team of experienced tax professionals and lawyers who are driven by a commitment to quality and client success. We combine specialized expertise in Pakistani tax law with broad experience across multiple industries.

Based in Taxila, Pakistan, our team has extensive experience representing clients before all major revenue authorities, including FBR, Punjab Revenue Authority, SECP, and various courts. We provide comprehensive services covering company registration, tax compliance, intellectual property, and regulatory matters.

Our skilled attorneys represent clients before Revenue Authorities, Commissioner Appeals, Appellate Tribunal, High Courts, and Supreme Court of Pakistan. We handle tax notices, audits, rectifications, appeals, and litigation matters with expertise and dedication.
