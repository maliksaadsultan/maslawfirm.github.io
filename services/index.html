Welcome to MAS Law Firm

Your Trusted Business Compliance Partner in Pakistan

MAS Law Firm provides comprehensive business and legal services including company registration, tax filing, regulatory compliance, intellectual property protection, and business setup solutions for clients across Pakistan.

At MAS Law Firm, we offer personalized tax consultancy services. We take the time to understand your issues and find the best solutions based on our expertise. Our tax advisory services cater to both residents and non-residents, as well as foreign companies operating in Pakistan.

MAS Law Firm professionals offer customized services, including Income Tax Return Filing, Sales Tax Registration, Sales Tax Return Filing, WEBOC Registration, tax planning, resolving tax queries, and working with the Income Tax Department to handle tax notices and audit compliance. We also manage tax refunds and assist with challenges during tax proceedings, including tax appeals and litigation.
